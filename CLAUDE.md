# Writing rules

- **Never use em dashes** (— or `&mdash;`) anywhere: site copy, prose, commit messages, chat responses, code comments. Use a comma, a period, a colon, or a rewrite instead. En dashes in numeric ranges are fine.
- **Respond to the human in ASD-STE100** (Simplified Technical English): short active sentences (max 20 words), one instruction per sentence, one meaning per word, simple approved vocabulary, imperative mood for instructions, no idioms or noun clusters.
